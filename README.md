# 👥 HR Workforce Insights Dashboard (python, excel and power bi)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing workforce data to understand employee attrition, workforce demographics, job satisfaction, and salary distribution.

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyze employee attrition.
- Explore employee demographics and age groups.
- Understand attrition across departments and job roles.
- Analyze salary distribution and overtime.
- Explore employee satisfaction and workforce trends.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Excel:** Data analysis and preparation.
- **Python:** Data cleaning and analysis.
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboard and data visualization.

## 📊 Key HR Metrics
- Total employees.
- Employee attrition count and rate.
- Attrition by department and job role.
- Employee distribution by age group.
- Salary distribution.
- Job satisfaction and overtime analysis.

## 💡 Business Value
The dashboard helps HR teams explore workforce patterns, identify areas with higher employee attrition, and support data-driven HR decisions.
